Privacy Policy

Wonderlode — last updated 20 October 2026

Wonderlode is a private tool that its owner runs on their own computer to prepare short videos and publish them to the owner's own YouTube channel, youtube.com/@Wonderlode. It is not offered to the public and is used only by the channel owner.

What Google data the app accesses

When the owner connects their YouTube channel, the app asks Google for permission to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel (YouTube Data API, scope youtube.upload);
  • read the channel's basic information — its ID, title and handle — to confirm the right channel is connected (youtube.readonly);
  • add captions and a thumbnail to the videos it uploads (youtube.force-ssl).

The app does not read the owner's private messages, comments, subscribers, watch history, email or contacts, and it does not access any other user's data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel at the times the owner sets, with the title, description, tags, captions and thumbnail prepared for each video, and to show the owner which channel is connected.

Where it is stored

The OAuth refresh token and the channel's ID, title and handle are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the app's local database. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's. Access tokens are kept in memory for at most one hour.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising, profiling or training models. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The data is kept while the channel is connected. Pressing "Disconnect YouTube" in the app deletes the stored token and channel data from the computer. Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google account's third-party access page.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
